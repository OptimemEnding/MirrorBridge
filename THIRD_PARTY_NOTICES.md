# Third-party components

MirrorBridge uses Flutter/Dart, Flutter's icon assets, Android platform libraries,
and their dependencies. `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt` contains the complete text
decompressed from the verified application's `assets/flutter_assets/NOTICES.Z`.
Preserve that text when distributing the source or application. The homepage's
license button displays Flutter's runtime license registry.

Android and build-tool dependencies also retain the notices shipped with their
own distributions. The bundled Flutter notice collection is not a certification
of every build dependency's licensing obligations.

Nikon and other camera brand names identify compatible devices. Their trademarks
belong to the respective rights holders. MirrorBridge does not represent those
manufacturers.

Project-specific permissions are described in `LICENSE`; third-party licenses
remain applicable to their respective components.
